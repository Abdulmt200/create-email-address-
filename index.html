Go to your phone's Email app and create a new account

Open the Gmail app on your phone. If you do not have it, open your phone's Email app.

  1. Tap the profile picture or account icon at the top.
  2. Select Add another account.
  3. Choose Google.
  4. Select Create account.
  5. Choose the appropriate account type and follow the instructions on your screen.
  6. Enter your own correct details and complete any verification requested by Google.
  7. When setup is complete, confirm that the new account appears in the app.

How to log out after creating an account

On most Android phones, logging out of Gmail means removing that Google account from the device. This does not delete the Google account itself.

  1. Open the Gmail app.
  2. Tap the profile picture at the top-right corner.
  3. Select Manage accounts on this device.
  4. Choose the Google account you want to remove.
  5. Tap Remove account.
  6. Confirm the action if your phone asks you to.

The wording may vary slightly depending on your phone model and Android version.
